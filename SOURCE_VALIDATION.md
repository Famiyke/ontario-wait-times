# Source validation boundary

Ontario Health's official public surgical results and method definitions:
- https://www.ontariohealth.ca/system/reporting/wait-times/wait-times-results
- https://hsihelp.ontariohealth.ca/hsi/About/About_the_Surgery_Wait_Times_Dashboard.htm

Ontario Health distinguishes referral-to-consultation **Wait 1** and decision-to-surgery **Wait 2**, and performance depends on procedure, priority, reporting period, and facility or region. Priority 1 emergency cases do not enter public wait-time comparisons. Its official data can also contain suppression and missing-data codes.

The hospital outcomes, region gaps, 90% compliance counts and charts in this repository derive from a generated illustrative dataset, not official Ontario Health hospital performance. They should not be used for referral or resource decisions.
