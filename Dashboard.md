# Ontario Surgical Wait Times | Demonstration Dashboard

> **IMPORTANT: SIMULATED DASHBOARD, NOT OFFICIAL HEALTH PERFORMANCE DATA.** The published Tableau visualization uses generated sample records and may display real province, region or facility names. Its percentages, rankings, time series and other figures do not represent verified Canadian health-system performance. Do not use it to compare actual facilities, guide referrals or make policy decisions. The Tableau Public visual itself still requires a prominent disclosure from the publisher.

> **Portfolio simulation.** Hospital-level data and comparisons in this project were generated for demonstration. Dashboard rankings are not official Ontario Health performance findings.

## Dashboard reference
[Tableau Public dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/ikenna.nwogu/viz/OntarioSurgicalWaitTimesHealthAnalyticsDashboard/ONTARIOSURGICALWAITTIMES-HEALTHANALYTICSDASHBOARD)

## Views illustrated
- Procedure and facility-level compliance summaries.
- Comparisons across modeled geographic categories.
- Multi-year wait-time trend visualization.
- Filtering for simulated sites below a specified benchmark.

## Interpreting the dashboard
The hospital list, rates and comparisons are constructed. Do not use this dashboard to assess or refer patients to real facilities. Source definitions, reporting cycles, case mix and wait-time benchmarks require verification before applying this method to actual Ontario Health data.
